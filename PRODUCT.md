# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Thrive Hub primarily serves internal operations staff and administrators managing customers, contracts, projects, affiliate resources, finance reconciliation, and BI data from desktop computers. Brand and channel users have narrower, permission-scoped workflows. Mobile use is secondary and focuses on viewing information, reviewing or approving work, and completing a small number of quick actions rather than entering complex contracts or financial records.

## Product Purpose

Thrive Hub brings alliance-marketing operations into one dependable workspace. It should help staff move from customer intake through contract execution, project delivery, reconciliation, and performance review without losing context or exposing data outside the user's permitted scope. Success means routine work is fast to understand, difficult to perform incorrectly, and easy to audit.

## Brand Personality

Professional, clear, efficient. The interface should feel calm and trustworthy under daily operational use, with direct language and predictable interaction patterns.

## Anti-references

- Decorative SaaS dashboards that prioritize visual effects over operational clarity.
- Consumer-style interfaces with oversized controls, excessive empty space, or playful motion that slows frequent tasks.
- Dense legacy admin systems with inconsistent buttons, improvised modal layouts, weak hierarchy, and hidden interaction states.
- Mobile layouts that merely shrink desktop tables and forms without prioritizing review and quick actions.

## Design Principles

1. Make the next valid action obvious while keeping destructive actions deliberate.
2. Preserve operational context across customers, contracts, projects, finance, and BI workflows.
3. Use one predictable interaction vocabulary across modules; the same action should look and behave the same everywhere.
4. Optimize desktop for sustained work and mobile for review, approval, and quick updates.
5. Communicate permission, validation, loading, empty, and failure states explicitly rather than leaving users to infer them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Interactive elements must support keyboard operation, visible focus, logical focus management, Escape-to-close where appropriate, reduced-motion preferences, sufficient contrast, and accessible names. Primary mobile touch targets should be at least 44 by 44 CSS pixels. Color must not be the only carrier of status or meaning.
